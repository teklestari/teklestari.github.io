FolderX Privacy Policy

Developer: Tekno Lestari · Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short: FolderX does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account.

1. Who we are

FolderX is an Android app made by Tekno Lestari, Malaysia. If you have any question about this policy, email teknolestari01@gmail.com.

2. Information FolderX uses, and where it stays

  • Your list of installed apps. FolderX reads the names and icons of apps on your phone so you can put them in folders, and to suggest folders by app type. This happens only on your phone. The list is never sent anywhere.
  • Your folders and settings. The folders you create, their names, the apps in them and your settings are saved inside FolderX on your phone.
  • Backups. When you back up, FolderX writes a file to a place you choose with Android's own file or folder picker. FolderX can only open that file, or the one backup folder you choose. If that folder is synced by another app (for example a cloud storage app), that app's own privacy policy applies to the copy it uploads.

FolderX does not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, messages or any content from inside other apps.

3. Purchases (FolderX Pro)

FolderX Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; FolderX never sees your card or payment details. The Play Store app on your phone tells FolderX whether you own Pro, and FolderX remembers that on your phone. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Permissions FolderX asks for

  • REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES: lets you start uninstalling an app from a folder. Android always asks you to confirm.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: redraws your FolderX widgets after the phone restarts, and keeps automatic backups on schedule.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: lets you buy FolderX Pro through Google Play.

FolderX does not have internet access, and does not use accessibility services, drawing over other apps, or device administrator access.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not share, sell or rent any information, because FolderX does not collect any.

6. Children

FolderX is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

7. Deleting your data

Everything FolderX stores is on your phone. Uninstalling FolderX, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it. Backup files you saved stay where you saved them until you delete them.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
